# Book Visualizer: 12 methods × 3 styles

**The Creating Brain: The Neuroscience of Genius — Nancy C. Andreasen**

This public demonstration library contains **36 English images**: the existing twelve illustrated originals, reused unchanged, plus twelve monochrome-ink variants and twelve editorial variants. Each method is shown in every style. Open any image at full resolution to compare text and detail.

| Style | Design approach |
| --- | --- |
| Illustrated | Expressive, colorful, format-adapted illustration and visual storytelling |
| Monochrome Ink | Pen-and-paper explanation, hatching, doodles, and line-weight hierarchy |
| Editorial | Typographic hierarchy, flat symbols, a clear grid, and restrained red accents |

The methods organize learning differently; styles change their visual presentation. The same thematic content basis is used across sets, with layout and occasional paraphrase adaptations. These samples demonstrate design and learning structure, not independent factual analyses of the book.

[Open the offline filterable gallery](index.html) after downloading this directory. [Inspect the coverage and provenance manifest](manifest.json). The images are outside the installable plugin archive.

## Content scope and review

Chapter order follows the [English edition catalog](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL3397205M/The_creating_brain). The full book was not supplied; this is not exhaustive text analysis. The chapter examples and lessons, fictional comic, practice workflow, checklist, poster expression, and 30-day plan are original adaptations. The mental-model cards are selected learning lenses, not an exhaustive author model catalog. No direct author quotations are used.

The generated variants were visually reviewed for their format structure, learning-item counts, key text, attribution, and caveats. Rejected trials that mixed unrelated formats were excluded. The final library keeps eight sketchnote takeaways, ten Q&A cards, six comic panels, six cataloged chapters, six selected concept cards, and thirty action-plan days in each style. Flowcharts retain their decision paths. Image-text rendering can still vary; report issues with the exact filename and plugin version.

The 36 images are generated demonstration assets approved for this public library; they are not book scans or pages reproduced from the book. Book and author identifiers do not imply endorsement. See `manifest.json` for file hashes and provenance.

## Try the presets

```text
/book:book The Creating Brain by Nancy C. Andreasen --style ink
/book:mindmap The Creating Brain by Nancy C. Andreasen --styles all
```

The first request produces 12 ink images. The second produces three mindmaps. An ordinary full pack remains 12 images; only `--styles all` expands a full pack to 36.
